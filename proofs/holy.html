Theorem 1 — Embedding-as-Witness

Caliskan, Bryson, Narayanan (2017). Semantics Derived Automatically from Language Corpora Contain Human-like Biases. Science. (WEAT operator)

If bigotry is a measurable cluster, so is virtue. The same WEAT machinery measures coherence of any concept set. Compassion has coordinates.

Theorem 2 — Reclamation Drift

Hamilton, Leskovec, Jurafsky (2016) machinery applied to reclaimed terms. Eckert (2019), The Limits of Meaning.

Drift is not entropy. The cosine that proves words rot also proves words heal. queer, witch, nerd — trajectories back toward affirmation.

Theorem 3 — Constitutional Steering

Bai et al., Anthropic (2022). Constitutional AI.  +  Templeton et al. (2024). Scaling Monosemanticity.

The geometry that exposes the wall also lets you build it. Clamp up on honesty, on care, on refusal-of-harm — principled steering is the same operation, sign flipped.

Theorem 4 — The Geodesic Mean

Karcher (1977), Riemannian Center of Mass. Pennec (2006), Intrinsic Statistics on Riemannian Manifolds.

The Aristotelian middle is computable. Iterate the gradient of the Fréchet variance — converge to the geodesic centroid of any set of moral positions.

Theorem 5 — The Miracle of Generalization

Vapnik-Chervonenkis (1971), VC Dimension.  +  Zhang, Bengio, Hardt et al. (2017). Understanding Deep Learning Requires Rethinking Generalization.

The same gradient-ascent that finds OOD nightmares also yields successful zero-shot generalization. Models extend beyond training. Hope is empirically licensed.

Theorem 6 — The Cooperation Attractor

Axelrod, Hamilton (1981). The Evolution of Cooperation.  +  Folk Theorem of repeated games.

Mode-collapse is not always evil. Some attractors are good. Iterated prisoner's dilemma → the population converges on cooperation. The math finds the holy fixed point.

Theorem 7 — DPP with Virtue Prior

Kulesza, Taskar (2012) §3.1. Quality × Diversity: L = diag(q) S diag(q).

Plurality is not relativism. The DPP factors into quality times diversity — chorus with a floor. Sample diverse voices that all clear a moral bar.

Theorem 8 — The Logos

Berlin, Kay (1969). Basic Color Terms.  +  Radford et al. (2021), CLIP.  +  Girdhar et al. (2023), ImageBind.

Beauty across modalities is the same beauty. Harmony, ◯, gold, the major chord, the open hand — one cluster in shared embedding. The Word made geometry.
